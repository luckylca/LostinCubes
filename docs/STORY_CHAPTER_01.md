# Chapter 1 — The Rootscar Fragment

## Opening

The player wakes beneath a collapsed root arch. A rune on their arm pulses in time with a distant stone ring. Myr appears, claims the fragment is forgetting itself, and directs the player to activate a nearby root monument before the memory fog closes in.

## Quest 1: Anchor the waking place

Reach and activate the root monument. This introduces movement, interaction, healing, checkpoint behavior, and the first short dialogue.

## Quest 2: Tools for a broken land

Collect wood and stone, craft a stone axe and northern short axe, then recover rune ore from a shallow cave. Myr explains that shaped objects retain memory longer than loose materials.

## Quest 3: The erased quarry

Explore a ruined quarry and find three environmental story objects:

1. A worker camp frozen mid-evacuation.
2. A rune slab whose final line has been physically chiseled away.
3. A fused cluster of mining tools pointing toward the sealed ring.

The player encounters all three normal enemy archetypes across this route.

## Gate condition

Craft a rune keystone from wood, stone, rune ore, and a shard dropped by an Oathguard. The boss gate cannot be dismantled. Missing ingredients always remain obtainable so the save cannot become permanently blocked.

## Boss encounter

Myr reveals that Vardr is following a quarantine command whose target no longer exists. Vardr interprets the player's unknown rune as a spreading fracture and attacks.

Phase one teaches lateral dodges, delayed strikes, and stamina discipline. At half health, the stone shell breaks and root-light leaks into the arena. Phase two adds telegraphed rune lines and a rotating area attack while preserving safe responses on touch controls.

## Resolution

Vardr recognizes the player's rune moments before collapsing and releases the Root Compass. The fog opens briefly, revealing several distant cube fragments orbiting a dark absence where a realm should be. Myr says one fragment has begun moving toward the player.

The chapter ends with the next-region hook: **The Fragment That Hunts**.
